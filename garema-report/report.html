01 Scope of Works Delivered

Works this period focused on the mechanical plant fit‑off: support steelwork, setting of Daikin VRV water‑cooled modules and ducted fan coils on anti‑vibration mounts, hanging of plenum boxes, and connection of insulated ductwork with dampers and access provisions.

  • Support steelwork — unistrut trapeze and goal‑post frames installed and levelled to unit footprints (PH-02, 03, 05).
  • Unit setting — Daikin RWEYQ‑TYM VRV modules and FXSQ ducted FCUs set on AV pads and hangers (PH-04, 06, 11, 15).
  • Plenums — boxes hung on threaded rod with AV hangers, safety wires and flexible connections (PH-10, 13).
  • Ductwork — insulated rectangular and spiral duct, transitions, VCDs and insulation made good (PH-08, 09, 12).
  • Coordination — supports set clear of hydraulic, fire and electrical services (PH-07, 13).
  • Materials — duct, dampers and fixings received, checked and staged in the basement store (PH-01).

03 Coordination & Observations

Open item — concrete anchors

Existing soffit anchors continue to be found stripped or not holding load. Anchors have been inspected and marked on the slab — compliant circled OK, defective marked X (PH-14). Replacement with HKD drop‑in anchors is recommended before further hanging; written direction is requested.

  • Vibration isolation — units set on AV pads or hangers; final levelling to be re‑checked after connection by others.
  • Insulation — insulation disturbed by supports temporarily made good; permanent reinstatement to be programmed (PH-09).

04 Safety & Compliance

  • All personnel site‑inducted; works under the site SWMS with daily pre‑start and toolbox attendance. Elevated work from approved platforms; metal cutting under permit.
  • No lost‑time injuries, incidents or environmental events recorded for the period.
