01 Scope of Works Delivered

Works this period covered the mechanical plant fit‑off — support steelwork, setting of Daikin VRV modules and ducted fan coils on anti‑vibration mounts, plenum hanging and insulated ductwork — together with the Ground Floor to Level 1 duct run and the material relocation directed by the supervisor.

  • Support steelwork — unistrut trapeze and goal‑post frames installed and levelled to unit footprints (PH-02, 03, 05).
  • Unit setting — Daikin RWEYQ‑TYM VRV modules and FXSQ ducted FCUs set on AV pads and hangers (PH-04, 06, 11, 15).
  • Plenums — boxes hung on threaded rod with AV hangers, safety wires and flexible connections (PH-10, 13).
  • Ductwork — insulated rectangular and spiral duct, transitions, VCDs and insulation made good (PH-08, 09, 12).
  • Coordination — supports set clear of hydraulic, fire and electrical services (PH-07, 13).
  • Ground Floor to Level 1 — duct runs installed and connected between GF and Level 1 to the revised drawings.
  • Materials — duct, dampers and fixings received, checked and staged in the basement store (PH-01).
  • Relocation — plant relocated to the rooftop store and balance covered, to supervisor direction (PH-16 to PH-22).

03 Coordination & Observations

Open item — concrete anchors

Existing soffit anchors continue to be found stripped or not holding load, and have been marked on the slab — compliant circled OK, defective marked X (PH-14). Replacement with HKD drop‑in anchors is recommended before further hanging.

  • Vibration isolation — units set on AV pads or hangers; final levelling to be re‑checked after connection by others.
